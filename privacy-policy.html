Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 20, 2026
App: AgentDock
Developer: Claudio Valenzuela / AISOSU
Contact: latribubooking@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how AgentDock handles information when you use the app. AgentDock is an AI developer tool designed to help users manage coding-agent workflows, repositories, tasks, approvals, and related development activity.

1. Information we collect

AgentDock may process information that you provide or connect to the app, including:

  • Account or authentication information, such as a GitHub login connection if enabled.
  • Repository metadata, such as repository names, branches, file paths, commit status, and task history.
  • Development workflow data, such as task descriptions, generated diffs, approval decisions, logs, and execution status.
  • Basic technical data, such as app errors, diagnostics, device or runtime information needed to operate and improve the service.

2. Information we do not intentionally collect

AgentDock is not designed to collect sensitive personal information such as government ID numbers, health information, biometric data, precise location, financial account details, or private personal messages unrelated to the development workflow.

3. How we use information

We use information only for legitimate product purposes, including:

  • Providing the core AgentDock functionality.
  • Connecting to repositories or developer tools when the user chooses to do so.
  • Displaying tasks, diffs, logs, approvals, and workflow status.
  • Improving app reliability, security, debugging, and user experience.
  • Complying with legal, security, and platform requirements.

4. AI and code-processing features

AgentDock may help coordinate AI-assisted development workflows. Depending on how the app is configured, task descriptions, repository context, file paths, code snippets, diffs, or logs may be processed by local or external AI/developer tools selected by the user or configured in the backend.

Users should avoid submitting secrets, passwords, API keys, private credentials, or sensitive personal data into prompts, repository files, task descriptions, or logs.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared only when necessary to provide the app functionality, such as with connected developer platforms, hosting providers, authentication providers, AI service providers, or infrastructure services used to operate AgentDock.

Information may also be disclosed if required by law, security obligations, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of the app, users, or third parties.

6. Data storage and retention

AgentDock may store task history, logs, repository metadata, approval states, and related workflow records for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain auditability, troubleshoot issues, or comply with legal and security obligations.

Users may request deletion of their data by contacting us at the email address listed above.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by AgentDock. However, no system is completely secure. Users are responsible for protecting their own accounts, devices, repository credentials, API keys, and connected developer services.

8. Third-party services

AgentDock may integrate with third-party services such as GitHub, hosting providers, analytics or crash reporting tools, authentication services, and AI/developer tooling providers. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Children’s privacy

AgentDock is intended for developers and professional or educational software-development use. It is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International users

AgentDock may be used from different countries. By using the app, you understand that information may be processed in countries where the developer, hosting providers, or service providers operate.

11. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal data. To make a request, contact us at: latribubooking@gmail.com.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as AgentDock evolves. When we make changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions, requests, or concerns, contact: latribubooking@gmail.com.
