AisoFlow Data Deletion Request

Effective date: May 23, 2026
App: AisoFlow
Developer: Claudio Valenzuela / AISOSU
Contact: claudio@aisosu.fi

This page explains how users can request deletion of data associated with AisoFlow. AisoFlow does not create separate in-app user accounts. Users may connect external developer services, such as GitHub, to use repository and workflow features.

How to request data deletion

To request deletion of AisoFlow-related data, send an email to: claudio@aisosu.fi

Please include:

  • The email address or GitHub account used with AisoFlow, if applicable.
  • A short message saying that you want your AisoFlow data deleted.
  • Any additional information that helps us identify the data related to your request.

Data that may be deleted

Depending on how you used AisoFlow, the deletion request may cover:

  • GitHub connection or session-related data, where applicable.
  • Repository metadata stored by AisoFlow.
  • Task history and workflow records.
  • Generated diffs, approval states, and execution logs.
  • Technical records directly associated with your AisoFlow usage.

Data that may be retained

Some limited technical records may be retained when necessary for security, debugging, abuse prevention, legal compliance, audit purposes, or to protect the integrity of the service. If any data is retained, it will only be kept for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

External services

If you connected AisoFlow to an external service such as GitHub, you may also need to manage or revoke access directly through that external service. AisoFlow cannot delete data stored independently by third-party services.

Processing time

We will review and process deletion requests within a reasonable time after receiving enough information to identify the relevant data.

Privacy Policy

You can also read the AisoFlow Privacy Policy here: https://aisosu.fi/privacy-policy.html
