Aurora Digital Assets

Institutional control layer for regulated digital asset operations.

Aurora is a deterministic prototype for risk review, compliance workflows, security guardrails, evidence packaging, agent governance and controlled human approval integrity before sensitive digital asset operations can proceed.

View architecture Start a conversation

Aurora is not a wallet. Aurora is a control layer.

The system is designed to sit between a regulated institution, compliance teams, risk operations and future regulated custodians. Its purpose is not to custody assets, but to make operations auditable, reviewable and controllable before execution.

What Aurora is

A bank-facing control layer for digital asset operations, approvals, evidence and review workflows.

What Aurora is not

Not a wallet, not an exchange, not a custodian and not a live banking integration.

Prototype boundary

Local deterministic backend prototype. No real assets, no real custody execution and no private keys.

Control architecture

Built around review before execution.

Aurora models the institutional controls required before regulated digital asset operations, cloud deployment paths or custodian integrations are allowed to proceed.

Public safety boundary
