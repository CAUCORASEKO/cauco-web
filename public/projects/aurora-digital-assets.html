Aurora Digital Assets

Institutional control layer for regulated digital asset operations.

Aurora Digital Assets is a local deterministic prototype for risk, compliance, security guardrails, evidence packaging and human review workflows around future regulated digital asset operations.

What Aurora is

A control layer designed to make digital asset operations reviewable, auditable and controllable before execution.

What Aurora is not

Aurora is not a crypto wallet, not a custodian, not an exchange and not a real bank integration. It does not move assets or store private keys.

Core modules

AI Threat Defense Secret Exposure Guard Sensitive Approval Guard Cloud / Kubernetes Guardrails Custodian Integration Guardrails Evidence Packages Review Lifecycle
Prototype status: local deterministic backend prototype. No real customer data, no real custody execution, no real bank connection, no real cloud deployment and no real regulated custodian integration.
